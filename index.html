02 / Why Kombu

A rhino doesn't reverse.

Kombu means horn. The rhino in our crest picks a line and charges. That's the deal here: choose a goal, then move toward it a little every day with a coach beside you.

  1. Coached, not just supervised

    Trainers work the floor, fix form and adjust your plan as you progress. Nobody gets left to figure out the machines alone.

  2. Room for every body

    First-timers, competitive lifters, women's groups, kids and parents train side by side. Leave the ego at the door.

  3. Progress you can see

    Check-ins, milestones and challenges give your training a scoreboard, so motivation doesn't have to do all the work.

06 / Questions

Before you walk in.

Still unsure? Call +91 95441 28666. A coach will pick up.

Where is Kombu Fitness located?

At Judgemukku, Thrikkakara in Kakkanad, Kochi (PIN 682021), close to Bharata Mata College. It's an easy ride from Edappally, Kakkanad and Infopark.

What are the gym timings?

Monday to Saturday 5:30 AM – 10:00 PM. Sunday 6:00 AM – 1:00 PM.

Do you have MMA classes in Kakkanad?

Yes. We run coached MMA and combat conditioning sessions next to the strength floor, and beginners are welcome.

I'm a complete beginner. Is that okay?

Absolutely. Coaches stay on the floor, set up your first plan and correct form as you go. Women, teens and older members train with us every day.

Can I try the gym before joining?

Yes. Message us on WhatsApp to book a free trial session.

How much is a membership?

Rates depend on the plan and on whether you add personal training. Message or call us for current pricing and offers.
